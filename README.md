# Simple Alarm

An iPhone and iPad alarm app with optional wake challenges.

## Privacy Policy

Simple Alarm is developed by Gonzalo Flores. This policy is effective November 20, 2026.

Simple Alarm has no account system, advertising, analytics SDK, or developer-operated server. The app does not send your alarm settings or challenge data to the developer, sell personal information, or track you across apps or websites.

### Data on your device

Alarm times, repeat days, morning messages, selected music identifiers, linked NFC text, settings, and active alarm or snooze state are stored on your device. Audio files you import are copied into the app’s storage. This data may be included in device backups according to your Apple settings.

### Optional permissions

Alarms and notifications: iOS schedules and delivers alarms and fallback notifications. You can change these permissions in Settings.

Camera: the face timer processes frames on your device to detect whether a face is present. Simple Alarm does not identify you, save camera images, or send them to the developer.

Microphone and speech recognition: used only when you choose to dictate a morning message. Apple’s speech recognition service may process audio off-device depending on your device and language. The resulting text becomes your morning message. Simple Alarm does not save the recording or send it to the developer.

Motion: movement and step counts are used for the current wake challenge and are not sent to the developer.

Music library: selected track identifiers and titles are saved so you can play music in the app. NFC: the text of a tag you link is saved locally; writing a tag stores your chosen text on that tag. NFC does not use GPS or track your location.

### Your choices

You can edit or delete alarms and disable challenges in the app. To clear a linked tag, use Clear Linked NFC Tag in Settings. Manage permissions in iOS Settings. Deleting the app removes its local data; manage device backups separately through Apple. Text written to an NFC tag remains on that physical tag until you overwrite or erase it.

### Apple services and external links

iOS, Apple Music, speech recognition, and device backups are governed by Apple’s terms and privacy practices. Opening the public policy or support page uses GitHub, whose own privacy practices apply. Avoid posting private morning messages, recordings, or NFC payloads in public support requests.

## Support

For help with Simple Alarm, open an issue in [GonzoRocket/Simple-Alarm](https://github.com/GonzoRocket/Simple-Alarm/issues). Include your iOS version and a description of the problem. Do not include private morning messages, recordings, or NFC payloads.

Alarms require Alarms permission. Select Open on a system alarm to begin your wake challenges. The system Stop control can dismiss the system alarm without a challenge. Custom music and spoken messages play while the app is open. Notification fallback may be silenced by Focus or system settings.
